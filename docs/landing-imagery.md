# Landing imagery and service gallery

Created 2026-09-04 with the built-in image_gen tool. The user approved the three product visuals and authorized publishing and creation of a separate horizontal gallery.

## Assets

- qr-live-queue: approved QR sign and mobile confirmation concept.
- multi-room-operations: approved three-room dashboard concept.
- hero-queue-studio: approved mobile queue concept, retained at the social-image URL.
- gallery-clinic, gallery-salon, gallery-service, gallery-specialist: four original illustrative service scenarios, not customer photographs or testimonials.

Gallery captions are HTML. All below-fold images have explicit geometry, lazy loading and 768/1536-pixel WebP sources. JPEG compatibility assets are refreshed as well. Product screens are explanatory concepts rather than exact app screenshots. QR patterns in these illustrations are not intended as usable entry codes.

## Interaction

Desktop windows at least 64rem wide and 50rem high use a sticky section: natural vertical page scrolling moves the image rail. Upward scrolling reverses the movement. The page never intercepts wheel or touch gestures. Arrow controls, arrow/Home/End keys and a skip link offer alternate navigation. Small or short viewports use a native horizontal swipe rail. Reduced motion shows all four scenes in a static grid, including when the preference changes during the session.

- Research Brain: AI-Assisted Premium Website Craft and Technical Standards — relevant media, consistent palette, AI artifact review.
- Research Brain: Website Interface Rules for Usability and Action — media explains the service and essential text remains readable.
- Research Brain: AI-Assisted Website Quality Scorecards, Checklists, and Redesign Corrections — product context rather than unrelated spa decor.
